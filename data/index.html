Word lists

The English and Hinglish lists inside Dono.

HomeHelpPrivacyWord lists

Dono's dictionaries are built from open sources. Some of them are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, so the lists derived from them are shared here under the same licence. Tab-separated, UTF-8, sorted by how often each word or pair is used (per million).

  • words-en.tsv: 120,000 English words
  • words-hi.tsv: 124,346 spellings of Hindi words in Latin letters
  • bigrams-en.tsv: 531,485 English word pairs
  • bigrams-hi.tsv: 600,000 Hinglish word pairs
  • LICENSES.md: every source, its licence and how it was changed
