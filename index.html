Dono

An iPhone keyboard for English and Hinglish.

HomeHelpPrivacyWord lists

Dono is for people who write English and Hindi in Latin letters, often in one sentence: "kal office mein meeting hai, will be late." It never turns your Hindi words into English ones. It learns the words, names and email addresses you type, and offers them again.

What it does

  • Suggestions in English and Hinglish, with a gentle autocorrect that fixes only clear English typos.
  • Glide typing: slide across the letters to write a word.
  • A number row, and symbols on a long press instead of accented letters.
  • Your own email, phone and address offered in the fields that ask for them, and names from the contacts you choose.
  • Emoji suggestions and your most used emoji; GIF search with KLIPY and GIPHY.
  • A list of what you copied, a calculator in the suggestion bar, light and dark looks.
  • Sync through your own iCloud. No account.

What any keyboard from the App Store can't do

  • Password and phone-number fields always use Apple's keyboard, and some apps turn other keyboards off.
  • GIFs are copied for you to paste; no keyboard can put an image into a message.
  • Haptics, key sound, GIF search, the clipboard list and sync need Full Access. Typing never does.
  • Voice typing is Apple's microphone key, below the keyboard.
